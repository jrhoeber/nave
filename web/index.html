Built for the Faith

Everything you need to pray together

Nave is designed for Catholics who know that faith grows in the company of others — praying, sharing, and walking the path together.

Prayer Groups

Join groups for the Rosary, Scripture study, men's and women's circles, and youth ministry — organized around the living traditions of the Church.

Parish Community

Stay connected to your home parish. Share reflections, follow announcements, and deepen the bonds with the people who share your pew every Sunday.

Group Messaging

Pray together in real time. Share intentions, offer encouragement, and respond to one another's needs — right within your group chat.

Prayer Requests

Never face a crisis alone. Send and receive push notifications for urgent intentions so your community can lift you up when it matters most.

The Church has always understood that faith is not a private affair. It is kindled and kept alive in the company of others — in the breaking of bread, in shared prayer, in the quiet witness of a friend who shows up. Nave is built on this conviction. Whether you are praying the Rosary with friends across the country, sharing a reflection after Sunday Mass, or quietly asking your community for prayers in a moment of need, Nave is your gathering place.
