# Nave — App Definition

## What is Nave?

Nave is a mobile app for Catholics who want to pray and stay connected with others in their faith community. It brings together people from the same parish and beyond, making it easy to organize prayer groups, share intentions, and support one another spiritually.

The name "Nave" refers to the central body of a Catholic church — the space where the faithful gather — reflecting the app's purpose of bringing people together in community and prayer.

IMPORTANT: Nave is owned an operated by a company called Cornerstone Dev LLC. Nave is not the name of the company, just the name of one of the applications the company owns. 

---

## Who is Nave For?

Nave is designed for practicing Catholics and those exploring the Catholic faith who want to:

- Stay connected with their local parish community
- Join or organize prayer groups (rosary, scripture study, men's groups, women's groups, youth groups, and more)
- Share prayer requests and spiritual reflections with trusted friends
- Find and connect with other Catholics in their area

The app is intended for adults and older teenagers. It is not designed for or marketed toward children under 13.

---

## Core Features

### Parish Community
Users choose their home parish when they sign up. This connects them to a community feed where parish members can post updates, share reflections, and engage with one another around parish life.

### Prayer Groups
Users can browse, join, and create small prayer groups. Groups can be public (open to anyone) or private (members must request to join and be approved by the group administrator). Each group has a dedicated chat room where members can communicate, share intentions, and organize.

### Group Chat
Each prayer group has its own chat space where members can send messages, react to messages with emoji, and stay in touch. Group administrators can manage membership and group settings.

### Friends
Users can search for other members by name or username, send friend requests, and build a personal network of friends within the app. The friends feature allows users to see and connect with people they know.

### Profile
Each user has a personal profile with a display name, username, and optional profile photo. Users can update their profile information and control their notification preferences.

### Push Notifications
The app sends push notifications to keep users informed about new friend requests, messages in their prayer groups, and other activity relevant to them. Users can control which notifications they receive and can mute individual groups.

---

## Data the App Collects

- **Account information:** email address, username, display name, and password (stored securely and never shared)
- **Parish affiliation:** the parish a user selects when signing up
- **Profile photo:** an optional photo uploaded by the user
- **Messages and posts:** content that users write and share within the app
- **Device token:** used solely to deliver push notifications to the user's device

The app does not does not track users across other apps or websites, and does not sell user data to third parties.
